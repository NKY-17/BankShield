# BankShield 🏦
### Banking Management and Fraud Protection System

BankShield is a web-based banking management system designed to simplify banking operations and support secure management of customer information and bank accounts.

## 📌 Project Overview

BankShield aims to provide a centralized platform for managing banking-related activities, improving operational efficiency, and supporting secure banking services.

Developed as an academic group project at BRAC University.

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. Customer Management
- Create new customer profiles.
- View customer information.
- Search for customers.
- Update existing customer details.

### 2. Account Management
- Create savings and current accounts.
- View bank account details.
- Freeze active accounts.
- Unfreeze frozen accounts.
- Prevent account closure when the remaining balance is non-zero.

### 3. Staff Authentication
- Staff login and logout.
- Staff-only access to Customer and Account Management.
- Prevent unauthorized access to protected management pages.

### 4. Saved Receiver Management
- Manage saved receiver information.
